A website asks for someone’s time and attention. Sending less, loading sooner, and staying out of the way are simple ways to respect both.

## Start with less

Static HTML is a good foundation for a content website. Give each page a clear purpose, size images for their actual use, and add JavaScript when it earns its place.

## Build for real conditions

Your audience may be on an older phone or a slow connection. Test beyond your own laptop. Read the page with a keyboard, reduce the viewport, and make sure the most useful content arrives first.

## Keep the habit

Performance isn’t a finish-line task. Check it as the site grows. Every new font, script, and image is a small decision about the experience you are offering.
