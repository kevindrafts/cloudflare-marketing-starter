---
title: "Design that leaves room"
description: "Why a little restraint makes the important things easier to see."
date: 2026-09-15
category: Design
---

A page doesn’t need to say everything at once. Good design makes space for a reader to find their way, notice what matters, and decide what to do next.

## Give the page a rhythm

Use a small set of type sizes and repeat spacing intentionally. Consistency helps people recognize the structure without having to think about it.

## Choose one next step

When every link competes for attention, the page feels unfinished. Decide which action matters most and give it a clear, descriptive label.

## Leave room to grow

Build a few useful sections rather than an endless library of variations. A simple system is easier to maintain, easier to understand, and easier to make your own.
